## Architecture and implementation decisions

The planned structure follows MVVM-style responsibilities: screens and reusable widgets display state, Cubits coordinate screen behavior, and repositories/API services provide product data. Constructor injection and immutable state keep the behavior testable.

The agreed libraries are Cubit through flutter_bloc, GoRouter, and Dio, introduced as they become necessary. The design system will use ThemeExtension tokens for light and dark themes.

The developer refined the sequence to establish an application shell, centralized route configuration, and a sample catalog screen before introducing theming and reusable components. Models, real data, state management, filtering, pagination, details, and adaptive layouts follow in small increments.

The plan records title-only case-insensitive search, single-category filtering, raw server pagination cursors, defensive parsing, USD pricing, and discount interpretation. These assumptions will be verified and refined during implementation.
